# csvdiff

[![build](https://img.shields.io/badge/build-passing-green)]() [![stars](https://img.shields.io/badge/stars-1k-blue)]() [![made with love](https://img.shields.io/badge/made%20with-love-red)]()

Welcome to csvdiff! csvdiff is a blazing fast, powerful and seamless next-generation solution that revolutionizes the way modern teams think about their data workflows. Born out of frustration during a long weekend of debugging, this project started as a small script and grew into something we are truly proud of. We believe data should be a joy to work with, and we hope csvdiff brings that joy to you and your team. Over the years many people have asked us how we manage our data comparisons and we always answered "with a little script", so finally we decided to share it with the world. It leverages state-of-the-art algorithms to deliver unparalleled performance at scale, and it integrates effortlessly into any stack.

## Philosophy

We think tools should do one thing well. We also think tools should be fun. csvdiff tries to be both.
