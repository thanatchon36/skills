# Social preview brief

- **Canvas:** 1280×640 PNG under 1 MB, with an 80 px safe margin on every side.
- **Name, large:** thanatchon-skills
- **Promise (one line):** From a one-page brief to a shipped public repo.
- **Snippet:** three short lines in a monospace block, copied from the real flow:

  ```text
  /mission-brief  →  MISSION.md
  /ship-oss       →  STOP GATE A: wait for "go"
  /launch-kit     →  launch/ (never posts)
  ```

- **Color:** one dark neutral background, one accent for the arrows and the gate line, contrast
  4.5:1 or higher. The text must be readable at 400 px wide.
- **Avoid:** fake UI, stock imagery, and any Anthropic or Claude logo. The image must not look
  like an official product.
- **Where to set it:** Settings → Social preview.
